sketchbook I


These are edited photographs of my sketchbook. I applied high contrast, reduced saturation, and topped it off with a photo negative filter. I hadn't drawn in the longest time and I wanted to start again.


krita


With my newfound drawing tablet and the power of Krita, I was able to utilize digital art to make something I was slightly more happier with. However, that bliss was shortlived as I come back to look at all the flaws. At that time, I had little to no experience with different colors and shading. Eventually, I lost interest and the confidence to keep going.


sketchbook II


Even though I was not in the right headspace, I occasionally snuck in a drawing or two into my sketchbook. Same methods were used to capture the art you see in "sketchbook I" with an optional "light" version.
